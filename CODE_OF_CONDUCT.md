# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and reviewers of PingPanda agree to make
participation in this project a harassment free experience for everybody,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps this project:

- Being friendly, patient and welcoming to newcomers.
- Being respectful of different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for the students who learn
  from this project.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies inside all project spaces - the repository, the
issue tracker and the pull requests - and also when somebody represents the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks this Code of Conduct, please
report it to the event organisers. All reports will be reviewed and handled
with respect for the privacy and security of the person who reported the
incident.

Organisers will take a fair and appropriate action in response to any
behaviour that they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
Depending on the situation, that can include a warning, a ban from the issue
tracker or a ban from the project.
